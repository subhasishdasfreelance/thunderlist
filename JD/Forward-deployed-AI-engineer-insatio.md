Job description
What you’ll do
Role Overview

The Forward Deployed Engineer is a hands-on, build-and-ship technical role that bridges deep engineering execution with product ownership. You will design, build, and deploy production AI-powered solutions that transform, owning the full path from requirements to a deployed, adopted system while also shaping what gets built through lightweight product ownership. This role is engineering-first: you spend most of your time architecting, coding, integrating, and deploying AI systems, and the remainder partnering with stakeholders to prioritize, validate, and drive adoption.


Key Responsibilities


AI Engineering & Solution Development

Design, build, and deploy production Generative AI solutions end-to- end.
Architect and implement on cloud infrastructure.
Build backend services and APIs.
Implement retrieval pipelines.
Build proofs-of-concept and MVPs rapidly to validate feasibility and business value, then harden them into reliable production systems.
Troubleshoot hands-on during implementation and deployment, and provide direct, technical problem resolution.

Deployment, Integration & Operations

Lead end-to-end deployment of AI solutions into live environments.
Configure, customize, and integrate AI agents with existing systems and workflows.
Containerize and operate services and ensure reliability and scalability.
Monitor solution performance and implement improvements based on usage data and feedback.

Governance, Quality & Evaluation

Build evaluation harnesses, guardrails, and human-in-the-loop controls to keep solutions safe, accurate, and compliant.
Ensure AI solutions comply with governance frameworks, security standards, and ethical guidelines.
Maintain quality standards throughout the development and deployment lifecycle.
Document technical specifications, user guides, and deployment processes.

Product Ownership & Stakeholder Engagement

Partner with client teams to understand pain points, gather requirements, and identify automation opportunities.
Shape and prioritize the product roadmap and backlog for AI solutions, aligning with business objectives and client needs.
Define user stories, acceptance criteria, and success metrics in collaboration with stakeholders.
Train and enable end-users, drive adoption, gather post-deployment feedback, and iterate.
Communicate progress, challenges, and outcomes to leadership and cross-functional teams.

Required Qualifications


Technical

1013 years in software engineering, solution architecture, or technical lead roles, including recent hands-on production Generative AI / ML work.
Strong programming skills in Python (JavaScript / Java); comfortable across the full stack.
Demonstrated experience building and deploying LLM applications: RAG, AI agents, agentic / multi-agent workflows, prompt engineering, and/or fine-tuning.
Hands-on experience with at least one major cloud platform.
Solid grasp of APIs, system integration, microservices, and data workflows.
Proven end-to-end ownership from requirements through architecture, build, deployment, and iteration.
Product & Domain

Experience translating business requirements into technical solutions and identifying automation opportunities in complex workflows.
Exposure to product practices: roadmap, backlog, user stories, acceptance criteria, success metrics and Agile delivery.
Excellent communication skills, with the ability to engage technical and non-technical audiences; client-facing experience preferred.

Technical Toolbox

LLM ecosystem: OpenAI / Azure OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, AWS Bedrock, Llama, Hugging Face, Azure AI Foundry
Agent & orchestration frameworks: LangChain, LangGraph, MCP
Retrieval & evaluation: Vector DBs (Pinecone/Chroma/Weaviate), RAG evaluation
Backend: FastAPI / Flask, Docker, PostgreSQL / MongoDB, microservices
AI-assisted SDLC: Claude Code, GitHub Copilot
Familiarity with AI governance, evaluation, and human-in-the-loop design.
