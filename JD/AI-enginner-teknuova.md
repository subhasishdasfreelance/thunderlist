What you’ll do
Summary of Responsibilities:

As an AI Engineer, you will apply your AI experience to build and deliver new AI driven features to production, with confidence in their performance. You will have an opportunity to work with other engineers and product stakeholders to refine feature concepts to best take advantage of opportunities afforded by AI techniques & models. Work with industry-leading engineering techniques and agent-first” ways of working to deliver your features and products, reflecting the realities of today’s software development lifecycle.

Detailed Responsibilities:

Build new products and features [80%]:

Contribute across the full software stack to deliver AI driven user journeys to production, contributing to implementation quality from architecture to release
Apply sound software engineering and system design principles to produce solutions that are observable, maintainable, scalable, and production ready
Build product features and agents that use frontier models with prompting, context, tools, and model reasoning to fulfill user journeys, generate content, and automate processes
Validate solutions against functional requirements using both traditional QA methods, evals, and tracing to ensure quality is measurable and reproducible
Use agentic software engineering techniques in the development workflow across the SDLC, from planning, design, implementation, testing, and maintenance
Actively participate in product definition as a technologist [20%]:

Participate in product refinement with designers and PMs: ideate, explore, and prototype along with stakeholders and other engineers to help arrive at deliverable, well-rounded solutions
Partner with other engineering team members in project scoping and prioritization
