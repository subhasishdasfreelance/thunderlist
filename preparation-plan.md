# ₹40LPA+ Senior Tech Job Preparation System

**Goal:** Prepare for ₹40LPA+ senior software roles through a **JD-driven, priority-based study system** focused on senior engineering, leadership, and product skills.

---

## 1. Start With the Market

### Collect 20 high-paying JDs

Collect **20 relevant senior-level JDs** for the kind of roles you want.

Extract every recurring requirement and turn them into a **master topic list**.

Focus especially on senior-level capability:

* **System Design & Scalability**
* **Architecture & Design Patterns**
* **Distributed Systems**
* **Backend & Databases**
* **DSA & Problem Solving**
* **Cloud, DevOps & Production**
* **AI / LLM Engineering & AI-driven SDLC**
* **Security & Reliability**
* **Leadership & Technical Decision-Making**
* **Product / PM fundamentals**

The goal is not to learn every technology mentioned. Identify the capabilities that repeatedly matter for senior roles.

---

# 2. Break Topics Into Manageable Units

Turn every major topic into smaller **subtopics that can be completed in a few study sessions**.

The result becomes your preparation backlog.

Each item should be small enough to:

**Learn → practice → review → complete**

---

# 3. Prioritize by Job ROI

Every topic receives one of four priority levels:

| Priority                  | Meaning                                           |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| 🔴 **Urgent + Important** | High job impact + needs attention now             |
| 🟠 **Urgent**             | Time-sensitive or blocking another important area |
| 🟡 **Important**          | Strong impact on senior-role preparation          |
| ⚪ **Normal**              | Useful, but lower immediate ROI                   |

Topics are always taken in this order:

**Urgent + Important → Urgent → Important → Normal**

Within each level, prioritize topics that appear frequently across the **20 JDs** and have the highest impact on senior-level interviews and work.

---

# 4. Track Every Topic as a Task

Every topic moves through exactly four states:

```text
TODO
  ↓
WORK OUT
  ↓
IN REVIEW
  ↓
DONE ✅
```

### TODO

Prioritized but not started.

### WORK OUT

Currently being studied and practiced.

### IN REVIEW

The batch has been completed and is waiting for review.

### DONE ✅

Reviewed, understood, practiced, and considered sufficiently complete.

This creates a visible measure of progress instead of relying on "I think I studied this."

---

# 5. Daily — 2 Hours

Do **not** study and review every topic individually.

Instead, work on a **small batch of related topics**.

### Daily routine

**First:** Pick the highest-priority unfinished topics from the backlog.

**Then:** Spend the 2 hours learning and working through several topics in the current batch.

The emphasis is:

> **Understand → investigate → code → design → solve → work it out**

Move completed study items from **TODO → WORK OUT**.

Continue working on the same batch across multiple days when necessary.

---

# 6. Weekend — Review the Batch

At the end of the week, review everything in **IN REVIEW**.

Test yourself without relying on notes:

* Can I explain it?
* Can I apply it?
* Can I solve a problem with it?
* Can I discuss trade-offs?
* Can I recognize when to use it?

Then move the topic to:

> **DONE ✅**

Anything that still feels weak goes back into **WORK OUT**.

---

# 7. Overall Strategy

The preparation should progressively build toward senior-level ownership:

**Solve problems → Design systems → Build systems → Scale systems → Operate systems → Make technical decisions → Lead execution**

Alongside technical preparation, continuously build:

**Leadership + communication + product thinking + technical judgment**

---

# Daily Rule

## **2 hours a day. Work on the highest-ROI batch.**

**20 JDs → Master Topics → Subtopics → Priority → Batches → Work Out → Weekend Review → Done ✅**

The system is complete when the **high-priority backlog is completed**, not when you have consumed a certain number of courses or study hours.
